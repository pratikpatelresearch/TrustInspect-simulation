Defensible manuscript positioning

TrustInspect is an auditable, risk-aware industrial inspection framework integrating anomaly evidence, normal-reference retrieval, structured multimodal reasoning, cross-evidence verification, selective automation, and explicit abstention. Its current novelty is system-level decision architecture and provenance—not proven detector-level SOTA superiority.
